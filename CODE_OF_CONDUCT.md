# Code of conduct

## Our standard

budiff welcomes everyone who wants to use it or improve it. Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Expected behavior includes:

- treating contributors and users with patience and professional respect;
- criticizing ideas and code, never people;
- acknowledging mistakes and uncertainty; and
- protecting private source, credentials, and personal information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, and repeatedly disrupting productive discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not put conduct reports in a public issue. Contact the maintainer privately through a channel listed on their GitHub profile. A report that also describes a security problem follows [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer reviews reports in good faith, protects the reporter's privacy as far as practical, and may edit or remove content, warn privately, limit participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Enforcement is proportionate and free from retaliation.
